How we decide a remote job is open to you

A job post that says only “Remote” does not tell you where you can work from, and many are limited by tax, legal or time-zone rules. We read the location the employer gives. A job counts as open to your country when it names your country, a region that includes it (such as Europe or Africa), or says you can work from anywhere.

A post that only says “Remote” and names no place is not matched to any country, so it will not appear on these pages. That keeps the lists honest, but it also means a few jobs that would accept you are left out. Counts are numbers of distinct listings on {{ PLATFORM }} today, not estimates.
