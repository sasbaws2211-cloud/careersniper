How it works

Job boards like LinkedIn and Indeed often repeat jobs that employers publish on their own career pages. Enter the title and company, and we look for the same role among the openings we read straight from that company’s career page. If it is there, you can apply on the employer’s own site instead of through a middleman, and you can see when we last saw the role open.

We are careful rather than generous: a company name has to match, and a role is only called a match when the title is close. When we are not sure, we say so and show the closest roles instead.

Questions

Why would the employer’s page be better?

You apply directly to the company, the posting is the original, and it usually shows whether the role is still open. Popular board listings can also be reposted or out of date.

It says it could not find the company. Does that mean the job is fake?

No. It only means the company is not one of the career pages we read. Check the company’s own website.

Do you store what I type?

No. The title and company are used to look up the answer and are not saved to an account.
