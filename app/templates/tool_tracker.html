What is in it

  • An Applications sheet with a row for each job: company, title, link, where you found it, the date you applied, the next step, who you spoke to and your notes.
  • A status list on every row: {{ statuses|join(', ') }}. Pick from the list instead of typing.
  • Follow-up reminders. A follow-up date that has arrived turns yellow while the job is still Applied or Interviewing.
  • Days since applied is worked out for you.
  • A Summary sheet that counts jobs by status and shows how many applications lead to interviews. It updates by itself.
  • A short guide on the last sheet, with tips on following up.

There are no macros and nothing to install. Room for {{ rows }} jobs is set up, and you can add more rows by copying the last one down.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to sign up for and no email address to give.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It is a normal spreadsheet file. Your data stays in your own file; we never see it.

How often should I follow up?

A common rule is about a week after applying, and again a week later. After that, move on and keep a note so you do not chase the same job twice.
