What we look at

  • Age. When the employer posted the role. Up to {{ limits.recent }} days is recent; beyond {{ limits.aging }} days many roles have been filled; beyond {{ limits.old }} days a posting is very old.
  • Reposting. Whether the role was closed and then posted again. Some companies do this to keep a role looking new, or to keep collecting résumés.
  • Last seen open. {{ PLATFORM }} re-reads company career pages regularly. If a role has not been on the page for more than {{ limits.gone }} days, it has probably closed.

We never call a job “fake”. We cannot see inside a company’s hiring, so we show what we can measure and let you decide.

Questions

What is a “ghost job”?

A posting that stays up with no real plan to hire, for example to collect résumés, look busy, or keep a role open for later. It is hard to prove from outside, which is why this tool only reports signals such as age and reposting.

Why can’t you check a LinkedIn or Indeed link?

Those sites copy listings from employers. We read the employers’ own career pages, so we can check the original posting if you give us its link, or the company and title.

Do you store the link I paste?

No. It is used to look up the answer and is not saved to an account.
