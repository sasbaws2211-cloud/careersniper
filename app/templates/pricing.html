FAQ

What if it is not for me?

Email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} within {{ REFUND_DAYS }} days of any payment and we refund it in full, no questions asked. Read the refund promise.

Do the plans renew automatically?

Weekly and monthly plans renew until you cancel from your account page, and you keep access to the end of the period you paid for. The 3-day pass and Lifetime are one payment and never renew.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. Browse a free preview of live jobs, then unlock the full results when you subscribe.

Which currency will I be charged in?

Prices are shown in US dollars. The amount actually charged is shown under each plan and again at checkout; other currencies shown are estimates, and your bank may convert the final amount.

What are job alerts?

Save a search (title, remote, location, filters). After you confirm your email, we send a digest when new matching roles appear.
