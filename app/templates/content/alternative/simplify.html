When Simplify is the better choice

  • You want one place for matching, tracking and networking, including help reaching recruiters.
  • You want a broader tool than a job search: its homepage also describes services for companies.

When {{ PLATFORM }} is the better choice

  • You want a search built around company career pages, with the freshness of each role and the pay ranges employers published.
  • You want to avoid roles that keep being reposted, which we flag.
  • You want a low-cost pass for a short search, instead of a subscription.
  • You want a single-purpose tool without the extra services.

What we could not confirm

  • How well its matching, résumé and networking tools work, which we did not test.
