When Himalayas is the better choice

  • You only want remote work. That is what it is built for, and we cover remote as one filter among many.
  • You want a free plan that includes a taste of AI tools, and a low monthly price if you pay: $9 a month for Plus is less than our ${{ CONSUMER_PLANS['monthly'].price_usd }} a month.
  • You want daily alerts and a large set of AI practice tools, such as headshots and spoken interview practice, in one subscription.

When {{ PLATFORM }} is the better choice

  • You want on-site and hybrid roles as well as remote ones, in one search.
  • You prefer to pay for a short stretch: a 3-day pass for $3, or a week for $7, instead of a monthly subscription.
  • You want to see when each role was last confirmed on the company's own page, and which roles keep being reposted.
  • You want salary pages built from the ranges employers published.

What we could not confirm

  • How Himalayas gets and refreshes its jobs, and how it handles closed ones.
  • Whether its job count or coverage is larger or smaller than ours in the area you care about.
  • The quality of its AI tools, which we did not test.
