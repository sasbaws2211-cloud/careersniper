When HiringCafe is the better choice

  • You want the widest possible search, and its stated size of over 5 million jobs is far larger than ours.
  • You do not want to pay before you have tried a tool: its homepage promotes free tools, and ours needs a plan for the apply link.
  • You want to browse a very large number of roles and are comfortable checking freshness yourself.

When {{ PLATFORM }} is the better choice

  • You want to know whether a role is still open: every job shows when it was last confirmed on the company's page.
  • You want to see roles that have been reposted, so you can avoid listings the company keeps open all the time.
  • You want salary information built from real published ranges.
  • You want short, low-cost access for an intensive search: a 3-day pass is ${{ CONSUMER_PLANS['pass'].price_usd }} and never renews.
  • You want help with the application itself: a résumé fit check, a tailored résumé, a cover letter and interview practice for the role you are looking at.
